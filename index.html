Why Founders Call Me

Founders usually call me when they’ve hit a "bandwidth wall." You have the tech and the traction, but you’re suddenly drowning in the business side—negotiating with enterprise legal teams, figuring out cross-border rules, or trying to turn a handshake into a signed contract.

With a background in both Business Operations and Law, I step in to handle that side of the house. I’m not just an advisor; I’m a senior operator who knows how to navigate the "messy middle" of scaling a company so you can get back to building the product.

The Fractional Commercial Lead

High-level seniority, without the full-time burn.

For high-growth scale-ups, I don't just advise—I step in. I act as a Fractional Commercial & Legal Lead on your leadership team (typically 1 to 2 days a week) for a flat monthly retainer. This gives you the senior expertise you need without the overhead of a full-time C-suite hire.

I take over the commercial pipeline to turn your tech into signed B2B contracts.

Commercial Contracting

(The Protective Railings)

A deal isn’t done until the contract is signed. I ensure those contracts protect you without killing the momentum.

  • Drafting Practical SLAs: Writing the commercial terms that define exactly what you guarantee to deliver, and strictly limiting your liability where you don't.
  • Managing External Counsel: I act as the "translator" between you and external law firms, ensuring you get the specific regulatory sign-off you need without the massive billable hours.
  • Negotiating Redlines: Handling the back-and-forth with enterprise legal teams to find the middle ground that actually gets the deal signed.

Meet the Founder

Cynthia Ajayi | Fractional Commercial Lead & Deal Architect

Cynthia Ajayi is a Commercial Strategist, Legal Operator, and Fractional Lead who builds the internal machinery for high-growth tech platforms.

With a career spanning over 7 years in legal and business operations, Cynthia’s expertise lies at the intersection of commercial scaling and risk management. Holding an MBA and called to the Nigerian Bar, Cynthia has a proven track record of architecting deals, building commercial contracts, and leading successful fundraising initiatives.

Cynthia's cross-sector experience is highly diverse, having structured commercial operations for fintechs, mobility companies, and scaling SMBs. Beyond the private sector, Cynthia has operated at the highest levels of institutional partnerships, consulting with government agencies and leading initiatives in partnership with the Netherlands Embassy in Nigeria.

Today, operating as a Fractional Commercial Lead out of the Lille tech hub in France, Cynthia leverages this wealth of experience to help technical founders bridge the gap between building a great product and executing a profitable, legally sound commercial strategy.
