Cynthia Ajayi

Founder & Principal Operator

Cynthia Ajayi is a strategic commercial operator and legal advisor dedicated to building sustainable frameworks for early-stage companies. With dual credentials as a qualified lawyer holding an LL.B degree from Joseph Ayo Babalola University, admission to the Bar of the Supreme Court of Nigeria via the Nigerian Law School, and a Master of Business Administration from IAE Lille, University School of Management, Lille, France, Cynthia brings over seven years of comprehensive professional experience to the firm.

Operating out of France, Cynthia integrates directly with global leadership teams to drive operational continuity and legal compliance. Rather than acting as a purely reactive external advisor, Cynthia functions as a fractional executive, managing day-to-day corporate governance, auditing accelerator residency agreements, executing share allotments, and structuring essential commercial Service Level Agreements (SLAs).

Beyond the boardroom, Cynthia is deeply committed to community building, cultural diplomacy, and prison advocacy, bringing a grounded, human-centric approach to high-stakes corporate execution.
